<p>wheee started from one question: what if both players shared the same piece of land, just from opposite sides?</p>

<p>You and your rival stand on two faces of one floating island. Raise a hill on your side and it becomes a pit on theirs. Each round you get a few quick moves, then the forecast comes true: wind pushes, rain floods the hollows, lightning hits the highest point. Whoever prepared worse goes over the edge or under the water.</p>

<h2>What is live today</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Real-time 1v1 matchmaking, with a bot stepping in when nobody else is around</li>
  <li>Invite links, so you can challenge a friend directly</li>
  <li>Win streaks, points and a shared leaderboard</li>
  <li>English and Russian</li>
</ul>

<p>The game runs on the same servers as <a href="https://wheee.io">wheee.io</a>, Telegram and Discord, so you may be matched against players from any of them.</p>

<h2>What I want to learn from itch players</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Did the first match make sense without reading How to play?</li>
  <li>When you lost, did you understand why?</li>
  <li>Would you play a second match, and what would make you come back tomorrow?</li>
</ol>

<p>Any answer in the comments helps, even one line. I'm updating the game weekly and will post here when something changes because of your feedback.</p>
