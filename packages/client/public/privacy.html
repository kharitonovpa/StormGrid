wheee — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

wheee is a free online game available at wheee.io, as a Telegram Mini App, as a Discord Activity, and on game portals. This policy describes what data the game handles and why. Short version: we keep as little as possible and sell nothing.

What we store

  • Account data (optional). If you sign in with Google or GitHub, or play inside Telegram or Discord, we store your display name, avatar URL, and the numeric ID the provider gives us — enough to show your name on the leaderboard. Inside Discord this is the identify scope, and it is the only scope we read your profile with. You can play without an account.
  • Match data. Results, replays, and win/loss statistics of the matches you play.
  • Gameplay analytics. Anonymous usage events (game opened, match played) keyed to a random device identifier. No names, no precise location — only a country-level code derived from your connection. We use this to understand whether players return, nothing else. Inside Discord the “game opened” event also records whether the activity was launched from a server or a direct message, the ID of that server, and — if you arrived through an invite — the ID of the player who shared it. These are Discord's own numeric IDs; we read them to see which communities the game reaches.
  • Local preferences. Sound settings and tutorial progress stay in your browser's storage (or the hosting platform's equivalent).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not send marketing emails or notifications.

Ads

On some game portals (e.g. Yandex Games, GamePush-hosted sites) the platform shows ads and applies its own privacy policy. The Telegram, Discord and wheee.io versions currently show no ads.

Data removal

To delete your account and its match history, contact us and name the account: pavel.kharitonov@unimatch.dev.
