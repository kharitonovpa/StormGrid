wheee — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

wheee is a free online game available at wheee.io, as a Telegram Mini App, and on game portals. This policy describes what data the game handles and why. Short version: we keep as little as possible and sell nothing.

What we store

  • Account data (optional). If you sign in with Google, GitHub, or play inside Telegram, we store your display name, avatar URL, and the numeric ID the provider gives us — enough to show your name on the leaderboard. You can play without an account.
  • Match data. Results, replays, and win/loss statistics of the matches you play.
  • Gameplay analytics. Anonymous usage events (game opened, match played) keyed to a random device identifier. No names, no precise location — only a country-level code derived from your connection. We use this to understand whether players return, nothing else.
  • Local preferences. Sound settings and tutorial progress stay in your browser's storage (or the hosting platform's equivalent).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not send marketing emails or notifications.

Ads

On some game portals (e.g. Yandex Games, GamePush-hosted sites) the platform shows ads and applies its own privacy policy. The Telegram and wheee.io versions currently show no ads.

Data removal

To delete your account and its match history, contact us and name the account: pavel.kharitonov@unimatch.dev.
